<objective>
Make the feed card actionable: implement the missing save/bookmark backend (SOCIAL-04) and build the on-card action rail (D-04) that wires like (SOCIAL-02), save (SOCIAL-04) and clone (SOCIAL-03) into the card built in 06-02.

Two of the three actions already have shipped, working backends — `POST`/`DELETE /api/trips/{id}/like` (SCRUM-161) and `POST /api/trips/{id}/clone` (SCRUM-162) merged 2026-08-06. Do not re-implement them. Save is the only genuinely new server-side piece, and it is a near-verbatim structural mirror of the shipped `trip_likes` feature: same composite-PK join entity, same `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` idempotency, same `loadVisibleTripLite` ownership gate. The one deliberate divergence is that saves do NOT denormalize a count column onto `trips` — nothing in D-04 or SOCIAL-04 displays a save count.

Applying `loadVisibleTripLite` to the two new save endpoints IS the SCRUM-274 resolution for them; the 404-vs-403 decision was made and centralized when likes shipped, so there is no design call left open here.

Purpose: SOCIAL-02, SOCIAL-03 and SOCIAL-04, delivered through D-04's rail.
Output: `trip_saves` table and endpoints, a saved-trips list endpoint, and a working three-button action rail on every feed card.
</objective>
